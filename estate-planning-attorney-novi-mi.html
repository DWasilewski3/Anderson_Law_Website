Estate & Life Planning

Thoughtful Estate & Life Planning Services for Novi Families

Anderson Law Firm, PLC helps Novi families put practical plans in place for incapacity, wealth transfer, and the people who will carry those plans out.

Novi grows quickly. Households arrive for work, settle near Twelve Oaks Mall or the Suburban Collection Showplace, and spend weekends at Lakeshore Park while careers and children keep the calendar full. Estate planning is easy to postpone in a city that still feels like it is being built, which is exactly why so many Novi families later discover that Michigan default rules do not match what they intended.

Anderson Law Firm, PLC serves Novi clients from Milford with plans designed for dual-income households, blended families, and owners of closely held businesses who cannot afford a gap in decision-making authority.

First-time plans are common here: a first house, young children, and no named guardian or financial agent if both parents are injured. So are updates for families who moved to Novi with out-of-state documents that no longer name Michigan agents or reflect Oakland County real estate. Life planning in this corridor is often about catching up to a life that changed faster than the paperwork.

Novi probate filings are made in the Oakland County Probate Court in Pontiac. For families who want to keep a home or business interest from sitting in that process, a funded living trust and current beneficiary designations matter more than a will sitting in a drawer. When court administration is already required, clear records and a realistic timeline make a difficult season more manageable.

If you live in Novi and have been meaning to put a plan in writing, start with the decisions that protect your family if you cannot act, then build the rest around how you actually want assets to pass.

Core Practice Areas

  • Planning for Incapacity Wills, powers of attorney, and decision-making if you cannot act.
  • Living Trusts Trust-based plans for privacy, control, and wealth transfer.
  • Planning for Business Owners Succession and ownership coordinated with your estate plan.
  • Probate & Trust Administration Guidance when a family needs to carry out a plan.
