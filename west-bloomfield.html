Estate & Life Planning

Thoughtful Estate & Life Planning Services for West Bloomfield Families

Anderson Law Firm, PLC helps West Bloomfield families put practical plans in place for incapacity, wealth transfer, and the people who will carry those plans out.

West Bloomfield planning often revolves around the lakes: Orchard Lake, Pine Lake, Cass Lake, and households where more than one generation has a stake in the same property. Those homes carry history, and they also carry title, taxes, and questions about who may stay, who may sell, and how to treat siblings fairly without forcing a sale.

Anderson Law Firm, PLC works with West Bloomfield families who need more than a simple will: trust terms that can hold a residence, protect a beneficiary, or coordinate lifetime gifts with what remains at death.

Multi-generational households are common here. Parents may still own the lakeside home while adult children live nearby and help with care. Charitable giving, retirement accounts, and closely held interests often sit alongside that real estate. Life planning in West Bloomfield is frequently about timing: when to transfer, when to retain control, and who can make medical and financial decisions if a parent’s capacity changes.

West Bloomfield probate, when it cannot be avoided, proceeds in the Oakland County Probate Court. A living trust that is actually funded, with deeds recorded and accounts retitled, is what keeps a lakes property from landing in that public process. If administration is already underway, the firm can help trustees and personal representatives understand their duties under Michigan law.

If your West Bloomfield plan still treats the family home as an afterthought, it is worth aligning the documents with how you want that property, and the people connected to it, to be treated.

Core Practice Areas

  • Living Trusts Trust-based plans for privacy, control, and wealth transfer.
