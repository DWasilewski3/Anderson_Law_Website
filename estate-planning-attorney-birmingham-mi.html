Estate & Life Planning

Thoughtful Estate & Life Planning Services for Birmingham Families

Anderson Law Firm, PLC helps Birmingham families put practical plans in place for incapacity, wealth transfer, and the people who will carry those plans out.

Downtown Birmingham moves at two speeds at once. Younger households buy near Shain Park, Maple, and Old Woodward while empty nesters downsize into condos they can walk from to dinner. Estate and life planning has to fit both: a first set of incapacity documents for someone still building a career, and a revised trust for someone who just sold the house where the children grew up.

Anderson Law Firm, PLC works with Birmingham clients who want plans that match a walkable, higher-value housing market rather than leftover documents from a prior suburb or another state.

Condo ownership, joint property with a partner, and retirement accounts that dwarf the equity in a smaller home are common facts on the ground. So is the mix of family structures: first marriages, later marriages, and adult children who may or may not live in Metro Detroit. Planning for incapacity is especially important for professionals who travel or live alone; a medical and financial agent needs to be named before an emergency, not after.

Birmingham probate, when required, is filed in the Oakland County Probate Court. A funded living trust can keep a condo, brokerage account, and personal property from becoming a public administration in Pontiac. If a family is already in court, the firm can help with the practical sequence of notices, inventories, and distributions under Michigan law.

If you live in Birmingham and your plan still describes a house you sold, or you have never named anyone to act if you cannot, it is time to bring the documents in line with the life you actually have downtown.

Core Practice Areas

  • Planning for Incapacity Wills, powers of attorney, and decision-making if you cannot act.
  • Living Trusts Trust-based plans for privacy, control, and wealth transfer.
  • Planning for Business Owners Succession and ownership coordinated with your estate plan.
  • Probate & Trust Administration Guidance when a family needs to carry out a plan.
